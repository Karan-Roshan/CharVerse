FAQ

What is this NFT collection about?

This collection celebrates creativity and individuality through custom-designed digital characters, each crafted with unique traits and metadata. Every NFT tells a story — and you own a piece of it.

How can I buy one of the NFTs?

You can purchase our NFTs directly on Opensea using Ethereum (ETH). Simply connect your wallet, browse the collection, and mint your favorite character before it’s gone.

What makes each NFT unique?

Each NFT features distinct artwork, metadata, and rarity levels — ensuring no two characters are alike. Your NFT is permanently recorded on the blockchain, making it verifiably unique and owned only by you.

Will there be physical collectibles?

Yes! Holders will gain access to exclusive physical merchandise and collectibles inspired by their digital characters. It’s a bridge between digital ownership and real-world expression.

How can I stay updated about new drops?

Follow us on Discord and LinkedIn for real-time updates, upcoming releases, giveaways, and community events. We’re building a creative space for collectors and fans alike.
